Meet Our CEO

A Message From Our CEO

Mrs. Theresa Yarry Kumchi — Founder & Chief Executive Officer
A healthy girl grows into a promised motherhood. When we remove the barriers around menstrual health, we give every girl the chance to stay in school and reach her full potential.

Teky Girlie Support Foundation was founded to educate and empower the grassroots girl child. Through menstrual health and hygiene support, education and advocacy, our team reaches girls in schools and communities across Jos, Plateau State — breaking the silence and stigma, and replacing them with dignity, confidence and equality.

We invite you to walk with us as a volunteer, partner or donor, so that no girl is left behind.

Our Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

A world where every girl has access to menstrual health and hygiene essentials, enabling them to reach their full potential, promoting dignity and fostering equality.

Our Mission

We aim to empower girls through menstrual health and hygiene support, education, and advocacy, breaking down barriers to their success.

Our Core Values

Empowerment

Dignity

Promoting dignity and self-worth among girls.

Equality

Education

Educating girls and communities about menstrual health and hygiene.

Compassion

Showing compassion and empathy towards girls in need.
